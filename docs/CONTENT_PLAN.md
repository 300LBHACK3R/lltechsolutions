## McKenzie launch angle

Lead with the work: custom website, real on-site photography and service video, ClinicSense booking connections, clear service/pricing content and Google launch support. Use Heather's exact approved testimonial. Do not advertise the historical $1,000 contract price as the agency's standard offer.

Suggested hook: “A website should feel like the business you walk into.”

Walkthrough structure: 0–5 seconds introduce the practice; 5–15 show real treatment-space imagery and the homepage; 15–25 show services and pricing; 25–35 demonstrate the ClinicSense link; 35–45 invite a similar project. Use actual screen recordings, not fabricated interfaces.

## Campaign discipline

- Make one claim and one next step clear in each post.
- Distinguish client work from L&L's own products.
- Show recorded metrics only with their date, device and measurement context. No ranking or revenue promises.
- Use current starting prices only where price is relevant; the old $400 promotion has ended.
- Track inquiry source and qualified conversations. Review what produced useful inquiries before increasing spend.
- Keep paid ad spend, platform access, audience selection and publication as separately approved actions.
